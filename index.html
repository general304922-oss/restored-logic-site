Refurbished business hardware  ·  Florida

We buy used business-class laptops, bring them back to working condition, and resell them to people who need reliable hardware without paying for new.

What we do

Corporate laptops leave their first life in bulk — lease returns, office refreshes, recycler pallets. Most are three to six years old, built to a far higher standard than consumer machines, and still have years of service in them. A missing drive, a dead battery or a wiped operating system is enough to move one from an asset to a write-off.

We buy those machines, assess them properly, restore what is missing, and put them back into use.

How it works

  1. AssessmentEvery unit is evaluated against current market data before purchase, so we know what a machine is worth and what it will cost to make it whole.
  2. RestorationDrives, memory, batteries and power supplies replaced as needed. Storage is wiped, a clean licensed operating system installed, and the hardware tested before anything is listed.
  3. Honest listingSpecifications are stated accurately and completely — the exact processor, memory, storage and graphics, and any fault we could not resolve. Buyers should know what is arriving.

Why used

A well-specified machine from 2019 outperforms most new budget hardware and costs a fraction of it. Keeping one in service for another few years is also the single largest reduction available in the footprint of a computer, most of which is spent before it is ever switched on.
